PROTECTION LAYERS

More than a single scan button.

AntiOS combines independent layers and reports their real state instead of collapsing everything into one fake score.

01

Managed ClamAV

Local malware scanning with managed signatures, bounded requests and explicit health reporting.

02

Resident Guard

Resident selected-folder monitoring and post-write scanning with visible degraded states and history.

03

Behavior signals

Correlation of suspicious activity as an additional signal — not a magic verdict and not a hidden destructive action.

04

Reversible quarantine

Explicit isolation and restore workflows with integrity checks instead of silent deletion.

05

Windows diagnostics

Defender state, Secure Boot, TPM, BitLocker, startup entries, disk space, pending restarts and system context.

06

Incident visibility

Local incident history and response tooling to understand what happened before taking action.
